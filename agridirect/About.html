Our Mission

AgriDirect SA was founded to build a fair, transparent, and efficient agricultural marketplace in South Africa. Our web platform connects emerging smallholder farmers directly with commercial buyers, ensuring fresher ingredients for kitchens and equitable profits for growers.

Empowering Local Farmers

Smallholder growers often face steep distributor markups and limited access to formal buyers. We provide a digital marketplace for transparent trading.

Streamlined Supply Chain

By shortening the supply chain from farm work to fork, produce reaches local kitchens quicker, cutting down food waste and guaranteeing peak quality.

Sustainable Local Economy

Keeping procurement within local farming communities helps strengthen the South African regional food system and supports rural economic growth.

Join The Movement

Whether you are a farmer looking to expand your buyer network or a chef looking for fresh farm produce, we are here to support your supply chain.
